## **Lancaster Chess Festival**

### **Monday 17 August in Market Square starting at 10 am**

It will feature learning to play, coaching to improve your game, blitz games, simultaneous sessions  with a FIDE Master and local experts.

There are giant chess pieces with support from CSC - https://www.chess-fest.com/lancaster. 

At 3 pm, the band “Pinsonic” will be doing a one hour set of live music featuring tracks with chess connected lyrics with equipment and set up through partnership with the Bobbin. 
 
Thanks to all who have volunteered to help in running the festival and Lancaster BID for their support.
