## **Coma Cup**

The annual Coma Cup between Lancaster and Penrith chess clubs took place in August. Lancaster had the better of the first tie, with Penrith taking honours in the return leg. The match ended up as a draw 8-8. All to play for in 2027!

Penrith visited Lancaster on 1st August for an 8-board tie with a time control of 110 + 10. Due to illness, only seven Penrith players made the trip and Lancaster ran out 7-1 winners.

- Max Fyson vs Alice Lampard ½ - ½
- Danny Mcmenamin vs Eggert Fruchtenicht 1-0
- Sam Barlow vs Cameron Savage 1-0
- Harrison Theobald vs Steve Murdock 1-0
- Haeysam Qaiser vs Andy McAtear 1-0
- Paul Jackson vs Grigorij Babin 1-0
- Jonathan Verden vs Dave Hood ½ - ½

On 8th August, Lancaster travelled tp Penrith with only six players. In the end, Penrith won the tie 7-1, leaving the match a tie.

- Alice Lampard vs Danny McMenamin 1-0
- Dave Siddall vs Richard Walsh 1-0
- Cameron Savage vs Paul Jackson ½ - ½
- Andy McAtear vs Dave Littler ½ - ½
- Grigorij Babin vs Jonathan Verden 1-0
- Richard Farrall vs Luca Holt 1-0

Well done to Luca for playing his first competitive game for Lancaster.
